## Quick Reference Guide

### Crisis Review Response (Within 24 hours)

1. ✅ **Acknowledge immediately** - Even if just "We've seen this and are looking into it"
2. ✅ **Move offline ASAP** - Provide direct contact
3. ✅ **Own it if appropriate** - Take responsibility
4. ✅ **Show action** - What you're doing to fix it
5. ✅ **Follow up publicly** - After resolution: "Thanks for speaking with us, we've addressed..."

### Daily/Weekly Habits

**Daily (5 min):**

- Check for new reviews on primary platform
- Respond to any urgent/negative reviews

**Weekly (20-30 min):**

- Full platform check
- Respond to all pending reviews
- Send review requests to satisfied customers
- Update metrics tracker

**Monthly (1 hour):**

- Deep analysis of patterns
- Template updates if needed
- Strategy refinement
- Team training/feedback

## Troubleshooting Common Challenges

### "I don't have time to respond to every review"

**Solution:**

- Start with negatives only (these matter most)
- Use templates to speed up positive responses
- Batch responses weekly instead of daily
- Delegate to trained team member with approval process

---

### "I'm afraid of saying the wrong thing to negative reviews"

**Solution:**

- Use Template 4 as your foundation
- When in doubt, lead with empathy
- Less is more - don't over-explain
- Move complex issues offline quickly
- Have someone else review before posting

---

### "We're not getting enough reviews"

**Solution:**

- Implement the review request system consistently
- Ask EVERY satisfied customer (most say yes!)
- Make it stupid easy - send direct link
- Follow up - one request isn't enough
- Create "review moments" in your customer journey

---

### "Competitor is leaving fake reviews"

**Solution:**

- Stay professional in public response
- Flag to platform with evidence
- Document patterns (timing, language, details)
- Focus on generating authentic positive reviews to dilute impact
- Never retaliate with fake reviews

---

### "Team responses sound inconsistent"

**Solution:**

- Create one-page voice guide
- Require template use
- Implement approval process initially
- Review and coach regularly
- Celebrate good examples
