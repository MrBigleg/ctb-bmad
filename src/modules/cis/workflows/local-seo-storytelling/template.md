## Content Creation Tips

### Writing Service Pages

**Do:**
✅ Write like you're talking to a friend
✅ Use customer language, not industry jargon
✅ Include specific local examples
✅ Show results and proof
✅ Make CTAs clear and easy
✅ Answer objections and concerns

**Don't:**
❌ Keyword stuff - write naturally
❌ Use generic stock photos
❌ Make it all about you - focus on customer
❌ Bury your contact information
❌ Forget mobile users

---

### Writing Blog Posts

**Structure:**

1. Hook them with a relatable problem or question
2. Break content into scannable sections with H2/H3 headings
3. Use bullet points and short paragraphs
4. Include images to break up text
5. Link to relevant service pages naturally
6. End with clear next step CTA

**SEO Essentials:**

- Target keyword in title, first paragraph, and 2-3 H2s
- Write 1,500-2,500 words for comprehensive topics
- Answer related questions in FAQ section
- Add internal links to service pages
- Use location keywords naturally when relevant

---

### Google My Business Posts

**Best Practices:**

- Post at least weekly for best visibility
- Use eye-catching emojis appropriately
- Include clear call-to-action
- Add relevant photo when possible
- Use local hashtags
- Keep text concise (under 300 characters ideal)
- Link to relevant page on your site

**Common Mistakes to Avoid:**

- Being too promotional all the time (mix in value)
- Forgetting to post regularly
- Generic content that could be anyone
- No call-to-action
- Broken or missing links

## FAQ - Common SEO Questions

### How long until I see results?

Local SEO typically shows initial results in 3-6 months:

- **Month 1-2:** Google indexes new content
- **Month 3-4:** Pages start ranking for long-tail keywords
- **Month 6+:** Compound effect - more content = more rankings

The key is consistency. Keep publishing quality content monthly.

---

### How often should I blog?

**Minimum:** 1 post per month (12 per year)
**Recommended:** 2-4 posts per month for faster growth
**Ideal:** Weekly posts if you have resources

Quality > Quantity. One well-researched 2,000-word post beats five thin 300-word posts.

---

### Do I need to write all this content myself?

Options:

1. **DIY:** Write yourself using these outlines (most authentic)
2. **Assisted:** Draft yourself, hire editor to polish
3. **Outsourced:** Hire content writer, but YOU provide details/examples
4. **Hybrid:** Write service pages yourself, outsource blog

Whatever you choose, inject YOUR voice and real examples.

---

### What if I'm not a good writer?

**You don't have to be!** Here's how:

- Use these outlines as fill-in-the-blank templates
- Write like you talk - conversational is good
- Focus on being helpful, not perfect
- Use Grammarly or similar for basic editing
- Have someone proofread before publishing

Your unique knowledge > perfect prose.

---

### How do I add content to my website?

Depends on your platform:

**WordPress:** Create new Page (for service pages) or Post (for blog)
**Wix/Squarespace:** Use built-in page builder
**Custom site:** May need developer help

If you need technical help, consider hiring a freelancer on Upwork or working with a local web developer.

---

### Should I update old content?

YES! Google loves fresh, updated content.

**Every 6-12 months:**

- Review top-performing pages
- Add new information and examples
- Update statistics and dates
- Improve based on new customer questions
- Add new testimonials

Mark updates with "Updated [Month Year]" at top of page.

---

### What about social media?

Social media doesn't directly impact SEO rankings, but it helps:

- Drives traffic to your website
- Builds brand awareness
- Can earn links when content is shared
- Google Business Profile IS important for local SEO

Focus on SEO content first, then amplify via social media.
